Hi, I am Joni Baboci — a planner, architect, builder and all-around urban enthusiast based in Tirana. I work where cities, public institutions, spatial data and technology meet.

My career has moved between public service, international urban-development work and building new tools for city-making. Across those settings, I have been interested in the same question: how can institutions understand complex places well enough to make better decisions, without trying to control everything that makes a city alive?

## Selected experience

### [Layer](https://getlayer.xyz)

**Founder · 2021—present**

I founded Layer as a spatial-data and artificial-intelligence platform for planning, governing and managing urban environments. It brings together geospatial information, urban indicators and decision-support tools so that teams can work across the usual boundaries between planning, mobility, infrastructure, climate and public finance.

### [World Bank Group](https://www.worldbank.org/)

**Consultant, Urban Planning and Resilience · 2020—present**

I advise on urban strategy, planning, resilience, climate adaptation and spatial data. My work has contributed to assignments across Eastern Europe, the Western Balkans, Africa and Southeast Asia — from Romania's national urban-policy work to city climate-finance support and the Malaysia Country Climate and Development Report.

## Earlier public work

I previously served as General Director of Planning and Urban Development for the [City of Tirana](https://tirana.al/), leading a multidisciplinary team of more than 100 architects, planners and engineers. Our work ranged from the city's [TR030 development framework](https://tirana.al/artikull/plani-i-pergjithshem-vendor) and electronic planning systems to public-space, school, mobility and major urban-regeneration projects.

Before Tirana, I led [Atelier Albania](https://planifikimi.gov.al/), an experimental planning and architecture unit within the Albanian government. I worked across national, regional and local scales, including Albania's national plan, the coastal regional plan and the Tirana–Durrës regional plan.

I have also collaborated with cultural and research institutions including Triennale Milano, and I studied architecture at the University of Toronto and cities at the London School of Economics.

Outside work, I like [cities](https://thinkthinkthink.substack.com/), [books](https://www.goodreads.com/dbaboci) and sharing half-formed thoughts online.
